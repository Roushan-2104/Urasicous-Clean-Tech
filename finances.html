Finances

Building the whole grid costs ₹36.1 lakh, and every rupee is listed below at today's market rates. Government subsidy and grants cover almost all of it, so each family pays one small amount, once. It's enough to make the grid truly theirs.

Market bill

Solar panels & generation40 kWp, 73 × 550 W modules, elevated mounting structures, DC wiring₹14,00,000
Battery energy storage90 kWh ₹12,00,000
Inverters & control (MGCC) and power conversion₹4,00,000
Wiring & distributionArmoured backbone cable, poles, service lines₹2,50,000
Household metering50 smart prepaid meters, MCBs, ELCBs, drop lines₹1,50,000
Safety & protectionMCCBs, fuse cut-outs, surge protection, lightning arresters₹1,20,000
Earthing & groundingPower house earth pits, pole earth rods, household electrodes₹90,000
Total gross capital cost₹36,10,000

We expect the grid to pay for itself in under 3 years, through money earned selling power back to the grid and the diesel families no longer have to buy. After that, export income goes straight into the maintenance fund.

Room to grow

Phase one is 50 homes, but we designed the grid so the rest of the village can join. Each step adds more panels over more fields, so farmland keeps growing food as the grid grows.
