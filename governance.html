Step 7 of 8

Run by the village

The grid belongs to the families it serves. An elected Village Electricity Committee runs it day to day, collects fees and sorts out faults, with a professional service contract to back them up.

The committee

  • ChairpersonGuides strategy and community coordination.
  • TreasurerHandles fees and keeps the public account books.
  • Lead technicianRuns weekly checks on the panels and batteries.
  • Two village representativesSpeak directly for the households.

Members are elected every two years by a show of hands at the village assembly. Each home pays a flat monthly maintenance fee, tiered by how much power it uses, collected in the first week of the month.

What happens during the monsoon or on cloudy days?

The battery always keeps some charge in reserve for dull days, and monocrystalline panels still catch plenty of light through cloud. During long grey spells, the battery tops up from the main grid whenever it's available. With AI Weather Forecast Mode on, the controller sees the clouds coming and saves extra charge a day ahead.

What if a family can't pay on time?

Smart prepaid meters simply pause supply when the credit runs out, so nobody piles up debt. If a family is going through a hard time, the committee sorts it out openly, neighbour to neighbour.

Who fixes breakdowns?

A local technician we've trained checks everything once a week. The big equipment is covered by a yearly maintenance contract with a professional service company. You can also reach our support team any time at 67811urasicousct@gmail.com.
