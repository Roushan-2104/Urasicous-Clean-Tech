The village & the need

Rampur Kheda is a farming village 38 km from Nashik. About 2,400 people live here in 480 households. They grow wheat and cotton, run a dairy, a flour mill and a handful of shops. When the power goes out, sometimes for 8 hours on a summer day, families who can afford it start up diesel generators and everyone else waits in the dark. We're starting with 50 homes on a grid the community owns, and we've designed it so the rest of the village can join later.

How we sized it

We sat down with five typical households and wrote down everything they run during a power cut. Then we scaled the average up to 50 homes and added 20% for the street lights and the shared water pump.

Average household need3,144 Wh / day
50 households157.2 kWh / day
+20% community loads31.44 kWh / day
Village requirement188.64 kWh / day

With about a day and 80% system efficiency, that works out to roughly 43 kW of panels. We went with and let the battery bank handle the evening rush.
