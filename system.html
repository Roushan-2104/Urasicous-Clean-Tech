How it works

Power flows one way: from the panels, through the power house, and out to every meter. Tap each stage to see the hardware we've picked, and tap any underlined word for a plain-English explanation.

Generate

Generate

Seventy-three 550 W monocrystalline panels on tall steel mounts about 3 m off the ground, wired in series-parallel strings and tilted 15° south to catch the sun all year. Crops grow in the shade underneath.

  • 73 × 550 W high-efficiency panels (40 kWp)
  • About 176 kWh on a clear day with 5.5 sun hours
  • DC cables run to the central power house
Store

Store90 kWh LiFePO4 BESS + MGCC

A battery bank saves up the extra midday sun for the evening and for every grid cut. The is the brain: it decides when to charge, when to draw down, when to go it alone and when to sell power back.

  • Round-trip efficiency above 92%
  • 6,000+ cycles at
  • , switch-over under 20 ms
Distribute

DistributeCables, meters and protection

Armoured cables run underground and along poles from the power house to every lane. Each home gets its own smart prepaid meter and , and lightning arresters and earth pits protect the whole network.

  • Armoured underground and overhead lines
  • 50 smart prepaid meters, one per home
  • MCBs, ELCBs and a full earthing system
